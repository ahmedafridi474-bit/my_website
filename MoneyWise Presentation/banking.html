BANKING 101

What is banking?

Banking is a service that helps people keep money, make payments and manage transactions. Banks offer different types of accounts and services. It is important to understand the terms, charges and safety rules before using any banking service.

Common banking services

  • Savings accountAn account for keeping money and building savings.
  • Current accountAn account often used for regular deposits and payments.
  • ATM cardA card that allows cash withdrawals from an ATM.
  • Online bankingDigital access to account information and transactions.

THINK LONG-TERM

What is investing?

Investment means using money to buy an asset or support an activity with the hope of earning a return in the future. Every investment carries some level of risk, and returns are not guaranteed.

Before investing, learn how it works, understand the possible risks and avoid decisions based only on promises of quick profit.
