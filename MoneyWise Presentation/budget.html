A budget is not about limiting your life. It is about giving your money direction.

THE BASICS

What is budgeting?

Budgeting is the process of planning how to use your money. It helps you compare the money you receive with the money you spend. When you make a budget, you can prepare for important expenses, reduce wasteful spending and save for future goals.

Five steps to create a budget

  1. Know your income. Write down the money you receive.
  2. Track your spending. Record where your money goes.
  3. Separate needs and wants. Give priority to essential expenses.
  4. Set a saving goal. Decide how much you can save regularly.
  5. Review your plan. Check your budget and make changes when needed.
✳

MONEYWISE TIP

Pay yourself first.

Try putting a small amount into savings as soon as you receive money, instead of waiting to see what is left.
